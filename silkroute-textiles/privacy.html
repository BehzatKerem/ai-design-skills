Privacy

This page is the public record of Silkroute Textiles, a limited liability company at Meydan Grandstand, Dubai.

The site stores no account and sets no cookie. A trade note is checked in your browser and kept in this tab only. It is not sent to Silkroute, and it is not sent to a server.

Close the tab and the note is removed. Refreshing the tab keeps it until you close the tab or choose to write another note.

If you write to the house later through a mailbox that is not on this page, that message sits outside this policy.
