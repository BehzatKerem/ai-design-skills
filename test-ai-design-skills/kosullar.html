Sample and order terms

This text is for a sample page. It is not a binding contract.

Metres

The lowest weaving order is 380 metres. A sample is cut at 4 to 8 metres, and that floor does not apply to the sample.

Sample fee

Cutting and shipping inside Turkey is 1,850 TL. Once the first invoice passes 380 metres, that amount comes off the invoice.

Shade

If the shipment report is above ΔE 1.2, we take the roll back within 15 days. Return freight is ours. On a dyed special shade, the promise depends on the laboratory dip you approved. On a repeat order the target is under ΔE 0.8.

Date

An approved sample leaves in 11 days. If yarn is late, the written note and the new day arrive in the same message. The dye line keeps a 4 day buffer.

Payment

A new buyer pays 40 percent when the order is confirmed, and the rest before shipment. After three orders close cleanly, 30 day terms open.

Papers

On sales to EU countries, the certificate of origin and the invoice are ours. The buyer chooses the customs broker.
